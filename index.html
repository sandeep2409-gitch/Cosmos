ABOUT COSMOS

COSMOS is an advanced 3D solar system exploration web platform built using Three.js, Vanilla ES Modules, custom GLSL shaders, and WebGL physics.

Experience photorealistic planetary atmospheres, solar plasma dynamics, 4K surface textures, real-time PCF soft shadows, and accurate Astronomical Unit (AU) orbital physics.

CONTROLS & SHORTCUTS

🖱️ Mouse Controls

  • Left Drag: Orbit camera around target
  • Right Drag: Pan camera position
  • Scroll Wheel: Zoom in / out
  • Click Object: Select celestial body & focus
  • Click Empty Space: Deselect object

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • W / A / S / D: Translate camera position
  • Space / Shift: Elevate / lower camera altitude
  • R: Reset camera to Solar System overview
  • ESC: Close active panel or modal
